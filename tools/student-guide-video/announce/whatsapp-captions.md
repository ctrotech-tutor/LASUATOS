# LASUATOS WhatsApp kit: captions

Everything here is ready to copy and paste. WhatsApp renders `*asterisks*` as
bold. Keep the link on its own line at the end so it turns into a preview.

Files in this folder:

- `LASUATOS guide - full (3 min).mp4` - the whole guide
- `LASUATOS guide - Part 1–4 … .mp4` - the short parts
- `WhatsApp square - student guide launch.jpg` - picture for the guide announcement
- `WhatsApp square - passkeys (staff).jpg` - picture for lecturers & admins
- `WhatsApp status - full guide (vertical).jpg` - full-screen status image

How to use: attach the video or picture, then paste the caption into the
caption box before sending.

## 7. Passkeys - for lecturers & admins (attach the passkeys graphic)

🔑 *New: sign in with your fingerprint or face*

Lecturers and admins can now add a passkey. Your phone or computer's own fingerprint, face or screen lock signs you in. No password to type, and a fake sign-in page can't steal it the way it steals passwords.

Set it up once: lasuatos.vercel.app → Account → Security → Passkeys

(You get an email whenever a passkey is added or removed, so your account stays yours.)

## 9. Personal email - link your Gmail (for students who can't open their LASU email)

📧 *New: use your Gmail with LASUATOS*

Can't open your LASU email? You can now link a personal email (Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook) to your LASUATOS account.

After linking:
✅ Sign in with Google using that email
✅ Codes and password resets go to the email you actually read
✅ Your LASU email stays your account email - nothing changes there

Link it once: lasuatos.vercel.app → Account → Personal email
New student? You'll see the option right after your account is ready.
