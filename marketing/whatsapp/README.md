# LASUATOS WhatsApp group kit

Everything needed to open the group and share it with every department.
Graphics are rendered into `out/`, which is not committed: the finished files are in the shared Google Drive folder "LASUATOS WhatsApp group kit". To re-render them with your real group link (adds a "scan to join" QR code), see the end of this file.

| File | Use it for | Size |
|---|---|---|
| `out/lasuatos-whatsapp-post.png` | Posting in department groups and DMs, with the caption below | 1080 × 1350 |
| `out/lasuatos-whatsapp-status.png` | WhatsApp Status (text kept clear of the top and bottom bars) | 1080 × 1920 |
| `out/lasuatos-whatsapp-group-icon.png` | The group photo (survives WhatsApp's round crop) | 640 × 640 |
| `out/*-qr.png` | Same, with a QR code: for printing, notice boards, and projector slides | |

## 3. Group settings (do these before sharing the link)

- **Group permissions → Edit group settings: Only admins.** Nobody can change the name, icon or description.
- **Send messages: All members.** It's a Q&A group. If it gets too noisy later, switch to Only admins and open it for set "question hours".
- **Approve new members: Off.** Joining should take one tap.
- **Add 3–5 admins** (you, plus trusted class reps from different faculties), so questions are answered across the day.
- **Disappearing messages: Off.** Old answers help new members.
- **Pin** the welcome message below, and later the most useful answers.

> Size limit: a WhatsApp group holds at most **1,024 people**. LASU has far more students than that. When you get close, turn it into a **WhatsApp Community**: one announcements group for updates that everyone receives, plus a help group per faculty. Then you can share one link that never fills up.

## 6. Ready replies for admins

Copy-paste these for the questions that will come up most often. They match the help centre.

**“How do I sign up?”**
> Open https://lasuatos.vercel.app/sign-up and enter your matric number. Your name fills in from your class list, a code goes to your LASU student email (yourname@st.lasu.edu.ng), and then you take three quick face photos. It takes about 2 minutes.

**“My matric number isn’t found / my name is wrong.”**
> Names come from your class list, which your HOC keeps on LASUATOS. Ask your HOC to add or correct you. It updates straight away, and then you can sign up.

**“I can’t open my LASU email.”**
> Ask your HOC for a setup code. They create it on the Class lists page and give it to you in person. Type it where the email code goes. It works for 30 minutes.

**“What’s my password?”**
> LASU + your matric number + your SURNAME in capitals, e.g. LASU250211184ODEJOBI. You can change it any time under Account. Forgot it? https://lasuatos.vercel.app/forgot-password

**“The QR code won’t scan.”**
> Type the code shown under the QR code at https://lasuatos.vercel.app/a. Live codes change every 30 seconds, so use the one on screen now. Wiping your camera lens helps too.

**“The face check keeps failing.”**
> Face a window or a light, hold the phone at eye level about an arm’s length away, keep your whole face in the circle, and turn your head slowly when asked. After two failed tries you can ask the person taking attendance to add you. More tips: https://lasuatos.vercel.app/help#q-face-fail

**“It got stuck on ‘All done · Checking your photos…’”**
> That was a bug and it’s fixed. Close the tab and open the class link again (pull down to refresh if the old page is still showing). Then try once more.

**“It says I’m outside the hall / asks for location.”**
> Turn on Location and allow it for this site. Android: tap the icon left of the address and allow Location. iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Safari Websites → While Using, with Precise on. Give it a few seconds to get a good fix.

**“My phone is dead.”**
> Borrow a classmate’s phone. After scanning, tap “A classmate without their phone? Let them sign in here” and sign in with your own details. It marks only you.

**“My attendance was removed / is missing.”**
> Speak to the lecturer or class rep who took attendance. They can see the entry and put it back in one tap.

**Anything private (account, data, a complaint)**
> Please don’t post personal details here. Use https://lasuatos.vercel.app/contact and the team will reply, usually within 2 working days.

## Re-rendering the graphics with your group link

Once the group exists, copy its invite link (Group info → Invite via link) and run:

```bash
cd marketing/whatsapp
GROUP_LINK="https://chat.whatsapp.com/XXXXXXXX" node render.mjs
```

This writes the `-qr` versions with a QR code that opens the group. The script needs `puppeteer-core`, `qrcode` and a Chromium (`CHROME=/path/to/chrome`). The photo is `src/hero.jpg`, and all text and layout live in `src/card.html`.
